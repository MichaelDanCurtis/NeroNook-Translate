# English reading script

Read each sentence once, naturally, at your usual pace, with a short pause after each. If you slip, carry on; do not restart the sentence.

## Scripted sentences

1. Thanks for joining, this is Michael Curtis on the line, and I think Ana Beltrán is dialling in from Seville.
2. We raised one point five million last quarter, not one point two, so the forecast needs to change.
3. Let's meet at ten thirty on Thursday the twenty-third, unless that's too early for you.
4. The order was for one thousand two hundred units, shipped on the fourth of March.
5. I don't think we should sign before the audit; actually, I'm certain we shouldn't.
6. Her number is zero seven five five, one two one, three three four; mine ends in nine eight.
7. We never agreed to a discount, we agreed to free delivery.
8. Sorry, I meant Tuesday, not Thursday, the fifteenth.
9. The invoice from Müller and Sons is due on the thirtieth of September, two thousand twenty-six.
10. It's not that it's expensive, it's that it isn't finished.
11. Send the draft to Priya Raghunathan and copy Tomás Oliveira.
12. The temperature stayed at minus four degrees for three nights in a row.
13. We'll need forty-five percent of the budget in the first half, or forty, at the very least.
14. I can't make it before noon, but any time after two works.
15. The flight leaves at six fifty-five in the morning from gate B twelve.
16. No, the deadline hasn't moved; it's still the first of December.
17. We tested version three point four point one, then rolled back to three point three.
18. There are eleven open issues, of which only two are blockers.
19. Please don't ship anything until Keiko Nakamura has signed off.
20. The meeting ran from nine to eleven fifteen and we covered eight of the twelve items.
21. If the price is above twelve dollars a unit, we walk away; below ten, we take all of it.
22. I said Lisbon, not Lisburn, the one in Portugal.
23. Half of the team is in Mexico City, the other half in São Paulo and Dublin.
24. That's two hundred and six, not two hundred and sixteen.
25. We'll decide on Friday; well, probably Friday, possibly Monday.

## Natural speech prompts

Talk for about four minutes in total, as if to a colleague. Pick two or three:

- What the next month looks like for you, with real dates and names where you can.
- Something you changed your mind about recently, and why.
- A plan with numbers in it: a budget, a trip, a schedule.
- A time a misunderstanding caused a problem, and how it was sorted out.
