# Synthetic test references

Three reference clips made with the macOS `say` command at 24 kHz, 16-bit mono, with their exact transcripts. They exist only to measure the engine (preparation time, switching overhead, first audio, real-time factor, cancellation). They say nothing about voice quality or identity; the real listening work in milestone 1d uses real recordings with their speakers' agreement.
