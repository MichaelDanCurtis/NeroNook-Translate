## Nero Voice and Agent identity

On 16 October 2026 the owner selected a soft, circular pearl-glass presence
with gentle wispy folds and fine flowing hair made entirely of separated dots.
The final refinement adds slight peach, lavender and ice-blue streaks within
the orb, keeping its body predominantly pearl and its dotted hair champagne.
Use this shared identity for Nero Voice and Nero Agent, with restrained poses
for rest, listening, thinking and speaking. Preserve the friendly softness;
avoid a hollow fiery ring, long isolated tentacles or saturated rainbow glow.
