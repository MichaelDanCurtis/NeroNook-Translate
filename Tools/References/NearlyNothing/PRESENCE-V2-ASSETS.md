# Presence and particle assets · October 18, 2026

Built-in image generation was used, with actual transparent backgrounds. Original
generated PNGs are copied without visual manipulation into the SDK resource
bundle. Each independent product bundles its own copy; no app reads another
app's installation. These assets do not give assistants an identity, account or
permission—the visible assistant name comes from the selected provider.

## Opal presence

`SDK/NookPluginSDK/Sources/NookPluginSDK/Resources/presence-opal-v2.png`

Mode: built-in image edit / background extraction. Reference:
`nero-presence-01.png`, the owner's approved identity sheet.

Final prompt specification: Create one faithful transparent production cutout of
the large upper-left/middle hero presence on the approved sheet. Preserve its
overlapping opalescent glass/silk folds, pale peach/champagne/lavender/ice-blue
highlights and flowing hair made of fine separate glowing dots. Keep all dotted
hair in frame, portrait composition. No text, UI, sheet, border or backdrop. Do
not simplify it to a pearl, blob, orb or ring.

## Latent-space particles

`SDK/NookPluginSDK/Sources/NookPluginSDK/Resources/latent-particles-v1.png`

Mode: built-in new image generation, transparent background.

Final prompt specification: A production wide approximately 2:1 transparent,
airy, wispy latent-space particle texture: a vast dense field of tiny discrete
points with occasional larger dots, curved filaments and eddies, cool pearl,
ice-blue and cyan, ample transparent negative space, fading at every edge. No
painted smoke, galaxy, broad gradient, mesh, sphere, UI or text.

The runtime gives the dense texture slow composited parallax. It does not claim
to simulate millions of individual particles. Reduce Motion stops movement;
Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast remove the decorative substrate.
